## Tire Compounds (2026 Pirelli)
- C1 (Hard) — White sidewall — Most durable, lowest grip
- C2 (Medium-Hard) — Used at high-degradation circuits
- C3 (Medium) — Yellow sidewall — Balanced performance
- C4 (Medium-Soft) — Used at low-degradation circuits
- C5 (Soft) — Red sidewall — Maximum grip, shortest life
- Intermediate — Green sidewall — Light rain
- Full Wet — Blue sidewall — Heavy rain
