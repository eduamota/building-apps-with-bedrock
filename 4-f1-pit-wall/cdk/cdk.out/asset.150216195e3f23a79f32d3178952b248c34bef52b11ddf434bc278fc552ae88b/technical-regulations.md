# 2026 Formula 1 Technical Regulations

## Power Unit Regulations (2026 New Era)

### Engine Specification
- Configuration: 1.6L V6 Turbo Hybrid
- Electrical power increased: 350kW from MGU-K (up from 120kW)
- MGU-H removed (simplification for new manufacturers)
- Power split: approximately 50% ICE / 50% Electric
- Fuel: 100% sustainable fuel mandatory
- Total power output: approximately 1000 HP

### New Manufacturers for 2026
- Audi (entering as Sauber power unit)
- Ford (partnership with Red Bull)
- Honda continues independently

## Chassis and Aerodynamics

### Active Aerodynamics (New for 2026)
- Adjustable front and rear wing elements
- Two modes: high-downforce (cornering) and low-drag (straights)
- Driver-activated system replacing DRS
- Designed to improve overtaking opportunities

### Dimensions
- Minimum weight: 768 kg (including driver)
- Car width: 2000mm maximum
- Car length: reduced from 2022 regulations
- Simplified floor design to reduce dirty air

### Ground Effect
- Venturi tunnels under the car generate majority of downforce
- Simplified compared to 2022-2025 regulations
- Designed to allow closer following

## DRS Replacement: Active Aero
- Replaces traditional DRS system
- Available in designated zones (similar to DRS zones)
- Rear wing flattens to reduce drag by approximately 50%
- Front wing adjusts simultaneously for balance
- Activation: within 1 second of car ahead (same as DRS rule)

## Safety Regulations

### Crash Structures
- Front impact structure: must absorb 780kJ
- Side impact structure: must withstand 25kN lateral force
- Halo device: mandatory titanium protection (since 2018)
- Roll hoop: must withstand 125kN vertical load

### Cockpit Safety
- Minimum cockpit opening dimensions enforced
- Driver extraction time: must be possible within 5 seconds
- HANS device mandatory
- Biometric gloves for driver health monitoring

## Tire Regulations (Pirelli)
- 18-inch wheels (since 2022)
- Tire allocation per weekend: 13 sets
  - 2 sets Hard, 3 sets Medium, 8 sets Soft (typical)
- Mandatory use of at least 2 different compounds in dry race
- Maximum tire sets in qualifying: 1 new set per session

## Parc Fermé Rules
- Car enters parc fermé after qualifying begins
- No setup changes allowed (except specific list)
- Allowed changes: front wing angle, tire pressures, brake bias
- Penalty for breaking parc fermé: pit lane start

## Penalties

### Grid Penalties
- Engine component change (beyond allocation): 5-10 place penalty
- Full power unit change: back of grid
- Gearbox change (before 6-race life): 5 places
- Impeding in qualifying: 3 places

### Race Penalties
- 5-second time penalty: minor infractions (track limits, unsafe release)
- 10-second time penalty: more serious infractions
- Drive-through penalty: significant violations
- Stop-and-go (10s): dangerous driving
- Black flag: disqualification

### Budget Cap
- 2026 cost cap: $135 million (excluding driver salaries, top 3 personnel)
- Minor breach (< 5%): financial penalty + minor sporting penalty
- Material breach (> 5%): significant sporting penalty (points deduction)

## Sprint Race Format
- Sprint qualifying: SQ1 (12 min), SQ2 (10 min), SQ3 (8 min)
- Sprint race: 100km (approximately 30% of full race distance)
- Points: 8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1 for top 8
- No mandatory pit stop in sprint
- 6 sprint weekends per season
