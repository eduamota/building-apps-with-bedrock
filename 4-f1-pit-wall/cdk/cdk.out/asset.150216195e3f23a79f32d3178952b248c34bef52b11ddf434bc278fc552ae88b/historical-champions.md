## All-Time Records

### Most World Championships (Drivers)
1. Lewis Hamilton — 7 championships (2008, 2014-2015, 2017-2020)
2. Michael Schumacher — 7 championships (1994-1995, 2000-2004)
3. Max Verstappen — 5 championships (2021-2025)
4. Juan Manuel Fangio — 5 championships (1951, 1954-1957)
5. Sebastian Vettel — 4 championships (2010-2013)
6. Alain Prost — 4 championships (1985-1986, 1989, 1993)

### Most Race Wins (All Time)
1. Lewis Hamilton — 104 wins
2. Max Verstappen — 63 wins
3. Michael Schumacher — 91 wins
4. Sebastian Vettel — 53 wins
5. Alain Prost — 51 wins
6. Ayrton Senna — 41 wins

### Most Pole Positions
1. Lewis Hamilton — 104 poles
2. Max Verstappen — 44 poles
3. Michael Schumacher — 68 poles
4. Ayrton Senna — 65 poles
5. Sebastian Vettel — 57 poles

### Most Constructor Championships
1. Ferrari — 16 titles
2. McLaren — 9 titles
3. Williams — 9 titles
4. Mercedes — 8 titles
5. Red Bull — 7 titles
6. Lotus — 7 titles

### Youngest World Champion
- Sebastian Vettel — 23 years, 134 days (2010)
- Max Verstappen — 24 years, 73 days (2021)

### Most Consecutive Wins
- Max Verstappen — 10 consecutive wins (2023)
- Sebastian Vettel — 9 consecutive wins (2013)

### Most Wins in a Season
- Max Verstappen — 19 wins (2023, out of 22 races)
- Michael Schumacher — 13 wins (2004, out of 18 races)

## Legendary Rivalries
- Senna vs Prost (1988-1993): Defined an era, multiple collisions, intense psychological warfare
- Hamilton vs Rosberg (2014-2016): Teammates turned rivals, Rosberg retired after winning
- Hamilton vs Verstappen (2021): Decided on last lap of last race, Abu Dhabi controversy
- Schumacher vs Hakkinen (1998-2000): Respect between fierce competitors
- Lauda vs Hunt (1976): Dramatized in the film "Rush", Lauda's fire crash and comeback
